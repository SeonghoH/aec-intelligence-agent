### Unveiling gauge-invariant on-chip phases from light intensity

- Source: arxiv
- Published: 2026-11-25
- Type: preprint
- Score: 20
- Tags: digital_twin
- Summary: Physics-informed digital twins fitted to output powers increasingly program photonic processors, but these powers do not fix the implemented phase, which determines how light from different inputs interferes. Here we show that a twin trained on intensities alone predicts the gauge-invariant phase of programmed transformations, and that this prediction can be used to program the phase.
- Why it matters: BIM-Digital Twin-AI 기반 의사결정 지원 시스템과 연결될 수 있음.
